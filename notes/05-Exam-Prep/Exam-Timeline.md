## How to use the app this sprint

1. **Read the lesson first, skim only** — each lesson is now a full study guide with tables,
   mnemonics, and sample questions. You don't need to memorize; you need to *recognize*.
2. **Take each quiz in practice mode** — instant feedback + rationales on every option.
   Anything you miss → note the objective and re-read that section of the lesson.
3. **Full exams under real conditions** — 90 questions, 90 minutes, no notes, no pauses.
   Target **≥ 83.3% (750/900)** on at least one full exam before exam day. The home-page
   readiness ring should read **"Exam-ready."**
4. **PBQs last** — do the PBQ set and flag PBQs inside full exams; they're the most
   time-consuming interaction type.
5. **Use the Weak-Topic Review** (due Aug 28) to close the biggest gaps, then rest.

## Readiness gates

| Gate | When | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | End of week 1 | ≥ 80% on both domain quizzes + checkpoint |
| G2 | End of week 2 | Full Exam #1 ≥ 75% |
| G3 | End of week 3 | Full Exam #2 ≥ 80% |
| G4 | Aug 28–29 | Full Exam #3 ≥ 83.3% AND readiness ring = "Exam-ready" |

If you hit G3 early, move G4 up and give yourself a buffer day — taking the exam a day early is
always better than taking it tired.

## Daily rhythm (26 days)

- **20–30 min/day**: one domain quiz or 20-question practice set in the app.
- **90 min, 3×/week**: full-length exam (Tue, Thu, Sat).
- **15 min/day**: review yesterday's misses + acronyms (the lesson-4-1 list has the full set).

## Exam-day checklist

- [ ] Photo ID + confirmation (test center) or clean room + webcam (online proctor)
- [ ] 90 questions / 90 minutes / 750 to pass — flag & skip, never leave blanks
- [ ] Multiple-select: match the count exactly ("Which TWO…")
- [ ] Rest the day before — light review only (ports, risk formulas, acronyms)
