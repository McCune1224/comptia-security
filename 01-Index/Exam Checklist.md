# Exam Day Checklist

## Before Exam (1 Week Prior)
- [ ] Scheduled exam date and time
- [ ] Confirmed testing center location or online proctoring setup
- [ ] Reviewed CompTIA ID requirements
- [ ] Tested computer/setup for online exam (if applicable)

## Day Before
- [ ] Light review only - no cramming
- [ ] Reviewed [[Weak Topics Tracker]] one final time
- [ ] Got good sleep
- [ ] Prepared ID and necessary documents
- [ ] Set multiple alarms

## Exam Day
- [ ] Valid ID ready
- [ ] Arrived 15-30 minutes early
- [ ] No phones/smart watches (leave in car/locker)
- [ ] Used restroom before starting
- [ ] Taken deep breaths and stayed calm

## After Exam
- [ ] Recorded score
- [ ] Updated study materials (if retaking)
- [ ] Celebrated! 🎉

---

## Required ID
- Primary: Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, etc.)
- Secondary: Additional ID with signature
- Name must match EXACTLY on registration

## What's Allowed
- Brain dump sheet (provided by testing center)
- Scratch paper/pencil (provided)
- Calculator (built into exam software)

## What's NOT Allowed
- Phones, smartwatches, tablets
- Notes or study materials
- Food or drinks (unless medically necessary)
- Hats or hoods (usually)

---

**Exam Details:**
- **Exam Code:** SY0-701
- **Number of Questions:** Maximum 90
- **Question Types:** Multiple choice + Performance-based (PBQs)
- **Time:** 90 minutes
- **Passing Score:** 750/900 (approximately 83%)
- **Cost:** ~$404 USD
